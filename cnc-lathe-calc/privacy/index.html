Privacy Policy

CNC Lathe Calc Android app (com.nettechdevelopers.cnclathecalc) · Last updated: November 11, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information the CNC Lathe Calc app ("CNC Lathe Calc", "the app", "we", "us", "our"), published by NetTech Developers, collects, how it is used and shared, and the choices you have. It applies to the app and to the services it connects to. By installing or using the app, you agree to this policy. Please also read our Terms of Service.

Summary

  • There is no sign-up or login. You can use the app without giving us your name, e-mail address or phone number.
  • Your programs, drawings, files and calculations stay on your device. We do not upload them.
  • The free version shows ads from Google AdMob. Ads and app analytics (Google Analytics for Firebase) collect device identifiers and usage data, as described below.
  • If you subscribe to Pro or redeem a code, the app contacts our licensing server to verify it and to keep it on one device at a time.
  • Surveys, feedback forms and profile details are always optional. You decide what to fill in.
  • We do not sell your personal data.

1. Information Stored Only on Your Device

The following is stored on your device and is never sent to us: the values you enter in calculators and generators, generated and edited G-code programs, your workspace (components, programs, drawings and other files), recently used tools, your language and theme, and other settings. Files that you open in the app from other apps, a file manager or a USB drive are read only to show, edit or simulate them. When you share or export a program, it goes only to the app or place you choose. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data deletes this information; keep your own backups.

2. Permissions and Device Features

  • Internet and network state – to load ads, check subscriptions, codes and forms, and to check for updates.
  • Vibration – for short feedback when you tap buttons.
  • Motion sensor (accelerometer) – only on the Precision Level screen, to show the tilt of the surface. Readings are not stored or sent.
  • Google Play Billing – to buy and restore the Pro subscription.
  • Advertising ID (Android and Google Play services) – used by Google's advertising and analytics services, see sections 4 and 5.

The app does not ask for, and does not access, your location (GPS), contacts, camera, microphone, photos, call log or SMS. The About and offer screens can open your phone, SMS, e-mail or WhatsApp app with our contact details filled in; nothing is sent until you send it yourself.

3. Information You Choose to Give Us

Surveys and feedback forms

From time to time the app may show a survey or feedback form. Answering is optional, and you can close the form. A form may ask for your opinion, a rating, your experience or profession, and sometimes contact or personal details such as your name, e-mail address or phone number. Questions that ask for personal details are marked or explained in the form. With your answers, the app sends the app version, the app language, your country (worked out from your network connection) and whether you use the free or Pro version.

  • If you use the free version, answers are sent without any account or device identifier. Only a one-way key, which cannot be turned back into a device ID, is sent so that the same phone is counted once per form. Answers to different forms are not linked to each other.
  • If Pro or a code is active on your device, answers are linked to your subscription record so we can help you.
  • Answers you mark as personal (such as e-mail or phone) are stored encrypted on our server.

Please do not enter passwords, payment card details or other sensitive information in forms.

Profile details (without an account)

The app may ask you to fill in an optional profile, for example your name, e-mail address, phone number, profession, company, or country, so that we can support you and improve the app. This does not create an account and is never required to use the app. Before you save personal details, the app tells you how they are used. Profile details are kept on your device and are sent to our server only when Pro or a code is active on your device. On our server, personal details are stored encrypted. You can change them, or erase them where the app offers “Erase my data”, at any time.

Creator offers and contacting us

If you take part in a creator offer, you send us links to your videos or posts by e-mail or WhatsApp. If you contact us for support, we receive what you send, such as your name, e-mail address or phone number and your message. We use this only to check your request, reply to you and send codes, and we keep it only as long as needed for that.

4. Advertising (Free Version)

The free version shows banner, full-screen (interstitial) and rewarded ads from Google AdMob. To show and measure ads and to prevent ad fraud, Google may collect your device's advertising ID, IP address, approximate location derived from it, device and app information, and how you interact with ads. Depending on your choices, ads may be personalised.

  • In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, Switzerland and US states with privacy laws, the app asks for your consent or gives you the opt-out required there before ads load. You can change your choice at any time in the app menu under Privacy settings.
  • On Android you can reset or delete your advertising ID, and opt out of personalised ads, in your device's settings (Settings → Google → Ads, or Settings → Privacy → Ads).
  • Pro subscribers see no ads.

See How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

5. Analytics and App Configuration

We use Google Analytics for Firebase to understand how the app is used and to improve it. It records events such as screens and tools opened, that a calculation was made or a program was viewed, copied, shared or simulated (not the values or the program itself), words typed into the app's tool search, the type of file opened (its extension, not its name or content), ad views and clicks, app update prompts, consent status, and your app language and theme. Firebase also collects an app instance ID, the device model, Android version, app version, and approximate location (country or region) from your IP address.

We use Firebase Remote Config to check whether your app version needs an update. It uses a Firebase installation ID. The app also uses Google Play's in-app update and in-app review features, which are provided by Google Play.

We do not use analytics data to identify you personally. See the Firebase privacy information.

6. Pro Subscription, Codes and Licensing

Pro is bought and managed through Google Play. We never see or store your payment card or bank details, or your Google account e-mail address. When you subscribe, start a trial, restore a purchase, redeem a code, or open the app while Pro is active, the app contacts our licensing server and sends:

  • Google Play purchase token and product (plan) – to verify the purchase with Google Play.
  • The code you redeem, if any – to check it and apply it to your device.
  • Device identifiers created for this app – a one-way hashed identifier derived from the device's Android ID (it cannot be turned back into the Android ID) and a device key created on your phone, to recognise this installation and keep Pro on one device.
  • Device model, manufacturer, Android version and app version – so you can tell your devices apart, and for support.
  • Google Play Integrity and key attestation results – to check that the app and device are genuine and to prevent misuse.
  • Device language and region, SIM country and time zone (only some of these, depending on the app's settings) – to show the right content and offers, and for support and statistics.
  • IP address (part of every internet request) – for security and abuse protection, and to work out your country. It is not saved to your subscription or profile record.

The app also downloads the app's settings, offers and forms from our server; for this it sends only the app's name and platform. A Pro licence is stored on your device so Pro works offline for a limited time.

7. How We Use Information

  • To provide the app's features and the Pro subscription you bought: verifying purchases and codes, unlocking Pro, enforcing the device limit.
  • To show ads in the free version and to measure them.
  • To understand how the app is used, fix problems and improve the app.
  • To run surveys and act on feedback.
  • To answer support requests and send codes you asked for.
  • To prevent fraud, abuse and misuse of the app and our server.
  • To meet legal, tax and accounting obligations.

Where data protection laws require a legal basis, we rely on: the contract with you (Pro and features you asked for), your consent (personalised ads where required, personal details in forms or profiles), our legitimate interests (security, fraud prevention, analytics to improve the app), and legal obligations.

8. Sharing

We do not sell, rent or trade your personal data. We share data only with:

  • Google – Google Play (billing, Play Integrity, in-app updates and reviews), AdMob (ads) and Firebase (analytics, Remote Config), as described above. Google processes data under the Google Privacy Policy.
  • Cloudflare – requests to our server pass through Cloudflare's network, which protects it against attacks and sees your IP address while doing so (Cloudflare Privacy Policy).
  • Our hosting and backup providers – who store our server's data for us under confidentiality obligations.
  • Authorities – when required by law, or to protect our rights, users or the public.

If NetTech Developers or the app is transferred to another owner, the data may be transferred with it, under this policy.

9. Data Retention

  • On your device – until you delete it, clear the app's data or uninstall the app.
  • Subscription, code and device records – while Pro is active and afterwards, so that you can restore Pro and for support and fraud prevention, until you ask us to delete them. We do not delete them automatically; you can have them deleted at any time (see Delete Your Data). Purchase records needed for tax or accounting are kept as long as the law requires.
  • Profile details – until you erase them or ask us to delete them.
  • Survey and feedback answers – as long as needed to analyse them, or until you ask us to delete answers that contain your contact details.
  • Analytics data – according to Google Analytics' retention settings (up to 14 months).
  • Support messages – as long as needed to handle your request.

10. Your Choices and Rights

  • Change your ad consent in the app menu under Privacy settings (where shown), and reset or delete your advertising ID in your device's settings.
  • Skip any survey, form or profile; leave out any optional question.
  • Cancel your subscription at any time in Google Play (Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions).
  • Ask us to access, correct or delete your personal data, to withdraw your consent, or to object to how we use it. See Delete Your Data. We reply within 30 days.

Depending on where you live, you may have further rights, for example under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, the EU/UK GDPR, or US state privacy laws, including the right to complain to your data protection authority. We do not sell personal data or share it for cross-context behavioural advertising other than through the ad services described in section 4, which you can control there.

11. Data Security

Data is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS). Purchase tokens and personal details on our server are stored encrypted, and access to them is restricted. The device key used for licensing is kept in your phone's secure hardware (Android Keystore). No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, but we work to protect your information.

12. International Transfers

Our server and our service providers may process data in countries other than yours, including India and the countries where Google and Cloudflare operate. We take steps so that your data stays protected as described in this policy.

13. Children's Privacy

The app is a professional tool for machinists, programmers and students. It is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from them. If you are under 18 (or the age of majority where you live), use the app with the consent of a parent or guardian and do not enter personal details in forms or profiles. If you believe a child has given us personal data, contact us and we will delete it.
