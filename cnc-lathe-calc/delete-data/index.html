Delete Your Data

CNC Lathe Calc by NetTech Developers · Last updated: November 11, 2026

CNC Lathe Calc has no user accounts. The data we hold about you is the record of your Pro subscription or code and the device that uses it, any optional profile details, and survey or feedback answers that contain your contact details. You can ask us to delete it at any time, without uninstalling the app.

How to request deletion

  1. Send an e-mail to developers.nettech@gmail.com with the subject "CNC Lathe Calc data deletion".
  2. Include what lets us find your data:
    • for a Pro subscription: your Google Play order ID (GPA.…, in the Google Play receipt e-mail or under Payments & subscriptions → Budget & history);
    • for a code: the code you redeemed;
    • for a profile or survey answer: the e-mail address or phone number you entered there.
  3. We may ask you to confirm the request from that e-mail address or phone number, to protect your data from others.
  4. We delete your data within 30 days and confirm by e-mail.

Where the app offers “Erase my data” on a profile, you can also erase your profile details yourself; this does not affect Pro.

What is deleted

  • Your subscription or code record (purchase token, plan, code and dates).
  • Your device records (device identifiers, device key, device model, Android and app version, language, country and time zone).
  • The Pro licences issued to your device.
  • Your profile details (such as name, e-mail address and phone number).
  • Survey and feedback answers linked to your subscription, and answers that we can find by the contact details you gave.

Deleting the subscription record ends Pro on our side for that device. It does not cancel billing: cancel your subscription in Google Play (Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions) first.

What is kept

  • Records we must keep by law, such as purchase records for tax and accounting, and our security log of past actions kept to detect abuse. After deletion these no longer link to your profile. Anonymous totals (for example, the number of subscriptions) may also be kept.
  • Anonymous survey answers from the free version that contain no contact details, as we cannot link them to you.
  • Your payment and order history stays with Google Play, which handles all payments; we cannot delete it. See the Google Privacy Policy.

Data on your device, ads and analytics

  • Your programs, drawings, files and settings are stored only on your phone. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data (Settings → Apps → CNC Lathe Calc → Storage → Clear data) deletes them.
  • Ad and analytics data is linked to your device's advertising ID and an app instance ID, not to your name. To delete or reset it, delete or reset your advertising ID in your device settings (Settings → Google → Ads, or Settings → Privacy → Ads) and clear the app's data. Analytics data is deleted automatically after up to 14 months.

You decide

We do not delete subscription, code and device records automatically when Pro ends, so that you can restore Pro or a code later. They stay under your control: you can ask us to delete them at any time, as described above.
