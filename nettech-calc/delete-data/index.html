Delete Your Data

NetTech Calc by NetTech Developers · Last updated: November 3, 2026

NetTech Calc has no user accounts. The only data we hold about you is the record of your subscription and the devices that use it. You can ask us to delete it at any time.

How to request deletion

  1. Open NetTech Calc and go to Subscription & devices.
  2. Copy the Support ID shown there.
  3. Send an e-mail to mr.saurabhwadekar@gmail.com with the subject "NetTech Calc data deletion" and your Support ID. If you cannot open the app, send your Google Play order ID (GPA.…) instead.
  4. We delete your data within 30 days and confirm by e-mail.

You can also remove a single device yourself at any time: Subscription & devices → Devices.

What is deleted

  • Your subscription record (purchase token, product and plan).
  • Your device records (installation ID, device key, device model, Android and app version).
  • The offline licenses issued to your devices.

What is kept

  • Our security log of past actions (for example "a device was activated"), kept to detect abuse. After deletion it no longer links to your subscription or devices. Anonymous totals (for example, the number of subscriptions) may also be kept.
  • Your payment and order history stays with Google Play, which handles all payments; we cannot delete it. See the Google Privacy Policy.
  • Calculations and history are stored only on your phone; uninstalling the app or clearing its data deletes them.

Without a request

Subscription and device records are deleted or anonymised automatically 180 days after your subscription ends.
