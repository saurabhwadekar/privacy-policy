Privacy Policy

NetTech Calc Android app (com.nettechdevelopers.example) · Last updated: November 3, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the NetTech Calc app ("NetTech Calc", "the app", "we", "us"), published by NetTech Developers, handles information when you use it. By installing or using the app, you agree to this policy.

Summary

  • The app does not ask you to create an account or sign in, and does not collect your name, e-mail address, phone number, contacts, location, photos or files.
  • Calculations and the calculation history stay on your device.
  • If you subscribe, the app contacts our licensing server to verify the purchase with Google Play and to keep the subscription limited to one device at a time.
  • The app shows no ads, and we do not sell or share data with third parties.

Information Stored on Your Device

Everything you type into the calculator and your saved calculation history are stored only on your device. They are never sent to us. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data deletes them.

Information Sent to Our Server

When you subscribe, restore a purchase or open the app with an active subscription, the app talks to our licensing server. It sends:

  • Google Play purchase token and product ID – to verify the purchase with Google Play.
  • A random installation ID and a device key created by the app – to recognise this installation. Neither contains personal information.
  • Device model, manufacturer, Android version and app version – so you can tell your devices apart in the app's device list, and for support.
  • Google Play Integrity and key attestation results – to check that the app is genuine and to prevent misuse.
  • IP address (part of every internet request) – for security and abuse protection.

You can use the free calculator without anything being sent to our server.

Payments

Subscriptions are bought and managed entirely through Google Play. We never see or store your payment details. Google's handling of payments is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

Service Providers

  • Google Play (billing, Play Integrity) – to process subscriptions and check app integrity.
  • Cloudflare – requests to our server pass through Cloudflare's network, which protects it against attacks and sees your IP address while doing so (Cloudflare Privacy Policy).

We do not use advertising or analytics services in this app.

How We Use Information

Only to provide the subscription you bought: verifying purchases, unlocking features on your device, enforcing the device limit, preventing fraud and answering support requests.

Data Retention

Subscription and device records are kept while your subscription is active and for up to 180 days afterwards, for support and fraud prevention. Then they are deleted or anonymised.

Your Choices and Rights

  • You can remove a device from your subscription in the app (Subscription & devices → Devices).
  • You can cancel your subscription at any time in Google Play.
  • To ask us to delete your subscription and device records, contact us with the Support ID shown on the app's Subscription & devices screen.

Data Security

All data is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS). Sensitive values such as purchase tokens are stored encrypted on our server.

Children's Privacy

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes are posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
