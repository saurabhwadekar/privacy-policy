Privacy Policy

HC Games Discord Bot · Last updated: October 6, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the HC Games Discord bot ("HC Games", "the bot", "we", "us") handles information when you use it on Discord. By adding the bot to a server or using it, you agree to this policy.

Summary

HC Games is built for playing games on Discord servers. We do not collect, store, sell or share your personal data.

Information We Collect

We do not collect or store any personal information. We have no database of users, messages or servers.

To run a game, the bot temporarily processes some information that Discord provides to it, such as:

  • Your Discord user ID, username and display name, to know who is playing
  • Server and channel IDs, to know where a game is running
  • Messages and commands sent in a channel where a game is active, to read game inputs

This information is kept only in memory while a game is running. It is discarded when the game ends or the bot restarts, and it is never saved to disk or a database.

Voice Channels

The bot may join a voice channel to run voice-based games or play game audio. HC Games does not record, store or analyze any voice or audio from voice channels.

Permissions We Use

The bot only asks for the permissions it needs to run games:

  • Connect / Speak (voice) – to join a voice channel for games
  • View Channels, Send Messages, Read Message History – to show games and read game inputs
  • Manage Messages – to clean up game messages (for example, removing old game prompts or inputs) and keep channels tidy

These permissions are never used for any purpose other than running games.

How We Use Information

Information processed by the bot is used only to provide its game features. We do not use it for advertising, profiling, analytics, or training machine learning or AI models.

Sharing of Information

We do not sell, rent, trade or share any information with third parties. The bot works through Discord, so your use of Discord is also covered by Discord's Privacy Policy.

Data Retention and Deletion

Since we do not store any data, there is nothing to retain or delete. Temporary game data is cleared automatically when a game ends. You can stop the bot from processing your information at any time by not using it, or a server administrator can remove the bot from the server.

Children's Privacy

HC Games is meant for Discord users only. Discord requires users to be at least 13 years old (or the minimum age in their country). We do not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes to This Policy

If the bot's features change in a way that affects your data, we will update this policy and change the "Last updated" date above. Continued use of the bot after changes means you accept the updated policy.
