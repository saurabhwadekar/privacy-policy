Privacy Policy

Metal Weight Calculator mobile app · Last updated: October 23, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the Metal Weight Calculator mobile app ("Metal Weight Calculator", "the app", "we", "us") handles information when you use it. It applies to the app on Android and iOS. By installing or using the app, you agree to this policy.

Summary

  • The app does not ask you to create an account, sign in, or enter your name, email, phone number or any other personal details.
  • We do not run our own servers or database, and we do not collect, sell or rent any personal information ourselves.
  • All weight and cost calculations are done on your device.
  • The app uses Google services – Google AdMob (ads), Google Analytics for Firebase (usage statistics), Firebase Crashlytics (crash reports) and Firebase Performance Monitoring (app speed). These services collect certain device and usage information automatically, as described below.

Information You Enter in the App

The shapes, materials, dimensions, quantities and prices you enter are used to calculate results on your device. We do not ask for, and you should not enter, any personal information in these fields.

To understand how the app is used, a summary of each calculation is sent to Google Analytics for Firebase as an anonymous event (see Google Analytics for Firebase). This summary contains the selected shape and material, material density, number of pieces, piece and total weight, price per kg and total cost. It does not contain your name or any contact details.

Information Stored on Your Device

The app saves your theme preference (light, dark or system) on your device so it is remembered the next time you open the app. This setting never leaves your device and is deleted when you uninstall the app or clear its data.

Third-Party Services

The app uses the following services provided by Google LLC. Each of these services collects information automatically when the app runs. We do not control what these services collect, and their use of the information is governed by Google's own policies:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • How Google uses information from apps that use its services
  • Privacy and Security in Firebase
  • Google AdMob – Privacy & messaging

Google AdMob (Advertising)

The app is free and shows banner ads served by Google AdMob. To show ads, measure them and prevent fraud, AdMob and its advertising partners may collect:

  • Your device's advertising ID (Android Advertising ID or Apple IDFA, where available)
  • IP address, which may be used to estimate your general location (such as country or city)
  • Device information, such as device model, operating system and version, screen size, language and mobile network
  • App information, such as the app's name and version
  • Ad interactions, such as which ads were shown and whether they were tapped

Depending on your choices and location, ads may be personalized (based on your interests, using the data above and data Google has from other apps and websites) or non-personalized (based only on general information, such as the content of the app and your approximate location).

Consent: If you are in the European Economic Area (EEA), the United Kingdom or Switzerland, or in another region where consent is required, the app shows Google's consent form (Google User Messaging Platform) before any ads are requested. You can accept or refuse personalized ads. You can change your choice at any time from Info (ⓘ) → Ad privacy settings in the app.

Google Analytics for Firebase (Usage Statistics)

We use Google Analytics for Firebase to understand how the app is used, which features are popular and how to improve the app. It collects:

  • An app instance ID, a random identifier created for each installation of the app
  • The advertising ID, where available and permitted
  • Device information, such as device model, operating system and version, language, screen resolution and mobile carrier
  • Approximate location (country, region or city) derived from your IP address
  • App information, such as the app version, first open time, number and length of sessions, and engagement time
  • Screens viewed in the app
  • App events, such as the app being opened, a shape or material being selected, a weight being calculated (with the calculation summary described above), the theme being changed, and an ad failing to load (with the error code)
  • Settings, such as the selected theme and whether ads are allowed under your consent choice

This information is used only in aggregated or anonymous form to see how the app is used. We do not use it to identify you personally.

Firebase Crashlytics (Crash Reports)

If the app crashes or hits an error, Firebase Crashlytics sends a crash report so we can find and fix the problem. A crash report may include:

  • Technical details of the error (stack trace) and the state of the app when it happened
  • Device information, such as device model, operating system and version, available memory and disk space, and device orientation
  • App version and whether the app was in the foreground or background
  • A Crashlytics installation ID, and the same anonymous app instance ID used by Google Analytics, so a crash can be linked to the related usage events
  • The last selected shape, material and theme, to help reproduce the error

Firebase Performance Monitoring (App Speed)

Firebase Performance Monitoring measures how fast the app starts, how smoothly screens are drawn and how long network requests take. It may collect performance timing data, device information (such as device model, operating system and version), app version, approximate location (country), network type, and a Firebase installation ID.

Google Fonts

The app uses fonts from Google Fonts. The first time the app runs, it may download font files from Google's servers and store them on your device. As with any internet request, Google receives your IP address and basic device information when the fonts are downloaded. No other information is sent.

How We Use Information

Information collected through the services above is used to:

  • Show ads, which keep the app free
  • Understand how the app is used and decide which features to improve
  • Find and fix crashes, bugs and performance problems
  • Check that ads load correctly and detect fraud or abuse

We do not use this information to identify you personally, and we do not combine it with any other personal information.

Legal Basis for Processing (EEA / UK Users)

If you are in the EEA or the UK, information is processed on the following legal bases:

  • Consent – for personalized ads, and for using your device's storage and identifiers for advertising, where required. You can withdraw consent at any time from the Ad privacy settings in the app.
  • Legitimate interests – for usage statistics, crash reports and performance data, which we need to keep the app working and improve it, and for non-personalized ads, which fund the app.

Sharing of Information

We do not sell, rent or trade personal information. Information is shared only with the Google services listed above, which act as our service providers (and, for advertising, may also use data under their own policies). We may also disclose information if required by law.

Under some laws (for example, in California), showing personalized ads can count as "sharing" personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising. You can stop this at any time by turning off ad personalization as explained in Your Choices.

International Data Transfers

Google may store and process information on servers in the United States and other countries, which may have different data protection laws from your country. Google uses appropriate safeguards, such as the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses, for these transfers.

Data Retention

  • We do not store any information ourselves.
  • Google Analytics event data is kept for the retention period set in our Firebase project (between 2 and 14 months), after which it is deleted automatically. Aggregated reports may be kept longer.
  • Firebase Crashlytics keeps crash reports and related identifiers for 90 days.
  • Firebase Performance Monitoring and Google AdMob keep data for the periods described in Google's policies linked above.

App Permissions

The app only uses the permissions needed for ads, analytics and crash reporting:

  • Internet and network state – to load ads, download fonts, and send analytics, crash and performance data
  • Advertising ID (Android) – used by Google AdMob and Google Analytics as described above

The app does not access your location (GPS), contacts, camera, microphone, photos, files or any other personal data on your device.

Your Choices

  • Ad consent: Where available, open Info (ⓘ) → Ad privacy settings in the app to change your consent choice.
  • Ad personalization (Android): Go to your device's Settings → Google → Ads (or Settings → Privacy → Ads) to reset or delete your advertising ID or opt out of personalized ads.
  • Ad tracking (iOS): Go to Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking to control whether apps can use your advertising identifier.
  • Google ad settings: Visit Google My Ad Center to manage how Google personalizes ads for you.
  • Stop all collection: Uninstalling the app stops all further collection of information by the app and the services above. It also deletes the theme preference stored on your device.

Your Rights

Depending on where you live (for example, under the GDPR in the EEA/UK or the CCPA/CPRA in California), you may have the right to access, correct, delete, or restrict the processing of your personal information, to object to processing, and to withdraw consent. Because we do not collect your name, email or any information that directly identifies you, we usually cannot link any data to you. If you want to make a request, contact us at the email below and we will help as far as we reasonably can, including by using the deletion tools Google provides for Firebase data. You also have the right to complain to your local data protection authority.

Data Security

Information sent to Google services is encrypted in transit using HTTPS. However, no method of sending or storing data over the internet is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

We do not knowingly collect information from children. If you believe a child has used the app and information about them has been collected, please contact us and we will take steps to delete it where possible.
